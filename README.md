# Downcount (v4 Edition)

Make a **live countdown** for the moments that matter, style it in the **Custom Theme Studio**, pick or upload your own **custom emoji stickers**, export **PNG share cards**, or **embed on your own website**. A tiny installable web app (PWA) — no accounts, no backend, everything stays on your device.

Based on [TheRealDev123/downcount-](https://github.com/TheRealDev123/downcount-).

## Core Features (Always On)

- **🎨 Custom Theme Studio (`#/themes` & Inline Modal)** — Design and save unlimited custom themes with solid or linear gradient backgrounds (`0°–360°` angle slider), custom text & accent colors, 4 digit typography options, optional **Neon Glow**, starter templates, and a **Randomize Harmony** generator. Custom themes carry right over into shared links and website embeds.
- **😀 Rich Emoji Picker & Custom Emoji Uploader** — Search 70+ curated emojis across 7 categories or **upload your own custom PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, or SVG stickers** (auto-scaled and saved locally in your browser).
- **📸 Export 1200×630 PNG Share Cards** — Download a crisp social share card image of any countdown with one click.
- **🔁 Recurring Countdowns, 📌 Pinning, Categories & Search** — Organize countdowns by category, pin top priorities, and repeat events daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.

## Opt-In Cool Extras (Turn On in `#/settings`)

All extra visual, productivity, and audio features are **OFF by default** so the app stays clean out of the box, and can be toggled ON individually (or all at once) in **Settings**:

- **🕰️ 3D Flip Clock, SVG Progress Rings & Tenths (`.0`)** — Unlock 3D mechanical flip cards, circular SVG gauges, minimal poster typography, and live tenths-of-a-second counters.
- **✨ Animated Particle Backgrounds** — Add interactive HTML5 canvas overlays inside countdowns (*Sparkles, Rising Orbs, Snow, Sakura Petals, Cyber Matrix Rain, Rising Embers*).
- **⏱️ Focus / Pomodoro Studio & Ambient Soundscapes (`#/focus`)** — Unlock the Focus timer tab with real-time Web Audio synthesized soundscapes (*Soft Pink Rain, Warm Harmonics Drone, Theta Binaural*).
- **✅ Event Checklists & 🌍 World Launch Timezones** — Add preparation checklists and global launch timezone comparisons to countdown pages.
- **🎆 Extra Finale Animations & 🔊 Celebration Sound Chimes** — Both extra finale effects (*Fireworks, Emoji Fountain, Cyber Strobe*) and synthesized celebration chimes (*Crystal Chord, 8-Bit Arcade, Royal Fanfare, Zen Bowl*) are **off by default** until turned on in Settings.

## Run or Deploy

Unzip the archive and upload all files directly to your [GitHub repository](https://github.com/TheRealDev123/downcount-) (and enable **GitHub Pages** on the `main` branch), or serve locally with `npx serve .` or `python3 -m http.server 8000`.
